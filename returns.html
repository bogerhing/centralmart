Return & Refund Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

Our Promise: We stand behind the quality of every product we sell. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.

1. Return Eligibility

Due to the perishable nature of grocery items, our return policy varies by product category:

  • Fresh Produce, Dairy, Meat & Seafood: Returns accepted within 24 hours of delivery for quality issues
  • Pantry Items (Non-perishable): Returns accepted within 14 days of delivery, unopened and in original packaging
  • Zero-Waste Products (Reusables): Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery, unused and in original packaging
  • Damaged or Incorrect Items: We'll issue a full refund or replacement immediately

2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Items that have been opened or partially consumed (for perishables)
  • Items past their expiration date
  • Custom or special-order items
  • Gift cards
  • Items damaged due to improper storage after delivery

3. How to Initiate a Return

1
Contact Us

Email or call within the return window

2
Provide Details

Order number, item name, and photos of issue

3
Get Approval

We'll review and approve your return

4
Receive Refund

Refund processed within 5-7 business days

4. Refund Processing

Once your return is approved, refunds will be processed as follows:

  • Original Payment Method: Refund issued within 5-7 business days
  • Store Credit: Available immediately upon approval
  • Partial Refunds: May be issued for items with minor quality issues

5. Quality Guarantee

We take pride in our fresh produce quality. If any item doesn't meet your expectations:

  • Minor Quality Issues: We'll issue a partial refund or discount on your next order
  • Major Quality Issues (spoilage, damage): Full refund or free replacement
  • Missing Items: Full refund for missing items + $5 store credit

6. Return Shipping

For returns of non-perishable items, return shipping costs are as follows:

  • Defective or Incorrect Items: Free return shipping (we provide the label)
  • Change of Mind: Customer responsible for return shipping costs

Note: Perishable items should not be returned by mail. Please contact us immediately for local collection or photo-based refunds.

7. Subscription Box Returns

For our Seasonal Harvest Box subscriptions:

  • Skip or cancel your next box up to 3 days before shipment
  • Returns on delivered boxes follow the standard perishable policy (24-hour window)
  • Subscription credits are transferable but not refundable after 30 days

8. Order Cancellations

You may cancel your order for a full refund if:

  • Cancellation requested within 1 hour of order placement
  • Order has not yet been processed for shipping
  • For subscription orders, cancellation available up to 3 days before next shipment

9. Contact Returns Department
