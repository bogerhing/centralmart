📖 Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking link via SMS/email. You can also visit 'My Orders' section after logging in.
What makes your packaging eco-friendly?
We use 100% compostable mailers, recycled paper padding, and glass jars. All plastic is eliminated.
Do you deliver across all USA?
Yes! We currently deliver to 12 states including CA, TX, NY, FL, IL, and expanding rapidly.
Can I partner as a local farm?
Absolutely! Email partnerships@centralmart.com with your farm details.
